From the [obituary]:

> Born and raised in Switzerland, Fred lived a remarkable life filled with
> adventure. His journey took him to England, where he met the love of his
> life, Gwen. Together, they returned to Switzerland, where they welcomed their
> daughter, Yvonne, before eventually settling in Canada, where their son,
> Drew, was born. Fred went on to enjoy a successful career as a gemologist, a
> profession that took him around the world and deepened his lifelong love of
> travel.
>
> Fred was known for his larger-than-life personality, unmistakable Swiss
> accent, quick wit, and cheeky sense of humour. He had a gift for making
> everyone feel welcome and bringing a smile to those around him. An avid
> traveller and devoted Chelsea Football Club supporter, Fred embraced life
> with enthusiasm. Retirement never slowed him down, as he remained physically
> active, participated in local clubs, and generously gave his time
> volunteering in the community.
>
> Above all, Fred lived 95 wonderful years filled with adventure, laughter,
> friendship, and family. His was a life well lived and deeply loved, leaving
> behind a beautiful legacy of memories, stories, and traditions that will be
> cherished and passed down for generations to come.
