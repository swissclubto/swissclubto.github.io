The Men's Section invites you, your family, and friends to a double feature
comparing how the Swiss and the Americans tackled a similar subject:

- [*The Little Rascals*][rascals] (1994), based on Hal Roach's *Our Gang*
  shorts from the 1930s and 40s, in English
- [*Mein Name ist Eugen*][eugen] (2005), recommended by many back home, in
  Swiss German

Even if you don't understand Swiss German, it is still worthwhile to come!

## Program

| Time                | |
|---------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| 11:00&nbsp;am            | Doors open                                            |
| 11:45&nbsp;am            | *The Little Rascals*                                  |
| 1:15&nbsp;pm             | Coffee and snack intermission: mingle, chat, and maybe sing some Swiss songs |
| 2:00&nbsp;pm             | *Mein Name ist Eugen*                                 |

As usual, baked goods are welcome! Please call [647-466-0523](tel:+16474660523)
to let us know if you will contribute, and to RSVP so we have an idea of how
many people will attend.

The York Woods Library is east of Jane Street, wheelchair accessible, and on
the Finch West LRT line.
