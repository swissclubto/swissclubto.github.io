---
title: Saengerfest 2027 in Ottawa
date: 2026-11-23 18:49:00 -0700
---

Save the date! Swiss choirs from across North America and Switzerland will
gather in Ottawa for **[Saengerfest 2027][sf]** from **July 1--4, 2027**.

Over four days, the festival will feature an opening banquet with fireworks,
competition singing, and a **Grand Concert** on Saturday, July 3, at the
Carleton Dominion--Chalmers Centre. It ends Sunday with an ecumenical service
and a closing brunch.

The participating choirs include the Montagna Singers of Ottawa, the Swiss
Yodelling Choir of Moorfield, the Swiss Choir Thames Valley, the Edmonton Swiss
Men's Choir, and Echo vom Lindenberg and Kapelle Oberalp from Switzerland, with
more to be announced.

The festival is supported by the North American Swiss Singing Alliance (NASSA),
the Montagna Singers, and local Swiss clubs. Details and updates are available
at <https://www.saengerfestottawa.ca/>.

[sf]: <https://www.saengerfestottawa.ca/>
